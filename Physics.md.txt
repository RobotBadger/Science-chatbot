Conservation of Energy
Energy cannot be created or destroyed.

Energy can only be transferred between stores.

Efficiency
Efficiency measures how much useful energy is produced.

Formula
Efficiency = Useful Output Energy ÷ Total Input Energy

Percentage Efficiency
Efficiency (%) = (Useful Output ÷ Total Input) × 100

Power
Power is the rate at which energy is transferred.

Formula
Power = Energy Transferred ÷ Time

Symbol:

P = Power (W)
E = Energy (J)
t = Time (s)
Equation
P = E / t

Electricity
Electric Charge
Charge is measured in coulombs (C).

Formula
Charge = Current × Time

Q = I × t

Current
Current is the rate of flow of charge.

Unit:

Ampere (A)
Potential Difference
Potential difference is energy transferred per unit charge.

Unit:

Volt (V)
Formula
V = E ÷ Q

Resistance
Resistance opposes current flow.

Unit:

Ohm (Ω)
Formula
V = I × R

Series Circuits
Properties:

Current is the same everywhere.
Potential difference is shared.
Total resistance increases.
Parallel Circuits
Properties:

Potential difference is the same across each branch.
Current is split between branches.
Total resistance decreases.

Internal Energy
Internal energy is the total energy contained within the particles of a substance.

It consists of:

Kinetic energy
Potential energy
Specific Heat Capacity
Energy needed to raise the temperature of 1 kg of a substance by 1°C.

Formula
E = m × c × ΔT

Where:

E = Energy (J)
m = Mass (kg)
c = Specific heat capacity
ΔT = Temperature change (°C)

Velocity
Velocity is speed in a given direction.

Acceleration
Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

Formula
Acceleration = Change in Velocity ÷ Time

a = (v - u) ÷ t

Newton's First Law
An object will remain at rest or continue moving at constant velocity unless acted upon by a resultant force.

Newton's Second Law
Force = Mass × Acceleration

F = ma

Newton's Third Law
For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Weight
Weight is the force caused by gravity.

Formula
Weight = Mass × Gravitational Field Strength

W = mg

On Earth:

g ≈ 9.8 N/kg

Momentum
Momentum is the product of mass and velocity.

Formula

p = mv

Measured in hertz (Hz).

Wave Equation

Np = Primary turns
